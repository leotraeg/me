Leonard Traeger

Research Associate in the Big Data Analytics group at Cologne University of Applied Sciences.

I work on data integration and language models for schema matching and dataset discovery. I build these techniques into open tools, benchmarks, and interactive systems, so that they are usable for people working with heterogeneous data every day.

I received my PhD in Information Systems from the University of Maryland, Baltimore County in 2026, advised by George Karabatis and Andreas Behrend. Before that, I completed my M.Sc. and B.Sc. at Cologne University of Applied Sciences and spent a semester at Macquarie University in Sydney. Outside academia, I worked for three years as a data warehouse consultant at BWI, building large-scale data pipelines and dashboard systems for the German Armed Forces.

When I am not at a desk, you will probably find me hiking, traveling, and playing chess.

Selected projects

Scoper

ICDE Demo 2026

Linkability assessment for schema matching: an interactive demo of our "Collaborative Scoping" (EDBT 2026) paper to narrow large schema collections down to the tables and attributes that are actually worth (linkability) matching.

  • Live app
  • GitHub
  • Paper
  • Video

RACT

arXiv 2026 · Technical report

Retrieval-augmented column–table learning for differently normalized multi-table schema matching, combining language-models with a self-supervised framework for cross-schema host table inference.

  • Paper
  • GitHub

TABERTA

EDBT 2027

A language model for dataset discovery, trained to encode tables in a way to support nearest neighborhood retrieval using schema co-occurrence information across large scale repositories.

  • Paper
  • Model

SEALM

ICEIS 2025

Semantically enriched attribute representations with generative language models to boost embedding similarities for schema matching across heterogeneous metadata privacy constraints.
